Support

Ran into an issue, or have a suggestion? We're here.

Frequently asked

My location looks wrong when adding a piece

Make sure location permission is enabled on your device: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → ArtFinder → "While Using the App".

My photo won't upload

Check your internet connection. Very large photos may take a bit longer to upload.

How do I delete my account?

Reach out at the email below and we'll delete your account and all your data.

Contact us

For any question, bug report, or suggestion: support@artfinderapp.com

We usually reply within 2-3 business days.
